About Fish Log Co.

Fish Log Co. is a mobile app designed for North Carolina anglers. Whether you're on the pier or in the boat, submit your reports in minutes and track your fishing journey over time.

We built Fish Log to make compliance feel less like paperwork and more like part of the trip — quick, clear, and worth doing.

NC Mandatory Harvest Reporting

Starting December 2025, North Carolina requires mandatory harvest reporting for five key species: Red Drum, Southern Flounder, Spotted Seatrout, Striped Bass, and Weakfish. All anglers who catch and keep any of these species must report their harvest. Fish Log Co. is the easiest way to stay compliant.
